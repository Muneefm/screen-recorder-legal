Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later. Recording a single window needs macOS 15.2 or later.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and click Enable screen access.
  2. macOS asks whether to allow screen recording. Choose Open System Settings, turn the app on under Screen & System Audio Recording, and choose Quit & Reopen if macOS asks.
  3. Choose Screen, Window or Region, check where recordings will be saved, and click Start recording.
  4. Stop from the floating bar, from the menu bar, or with Shift-Command-Period (⇧⌘.).

Common questions

Where are my recordings?

In Movies › Recordings, unless you chose another folder. Click the folder icon at the top of the window, or choose Open recordings folder in the menu bar. After each recording is saved, Finder opens with the video selected.

How do I record sound?

Before you start, turn on System audio (called App audio when recording a window), Microphone, or both. When recording a window, the sound comes from that window’s app, which can include its other windows.

How do I use it with an AI assistant?

Open the app’s Settings, go to AI assistants, turn on Allow local automation, then click Copy setup prompt and paste it into your AI app. Restart the AI app when it asks. Turning automation off ends any recording an assistant started. You can always stop a recording from the app yourself.

Troubleshooting

Nothing happened when I clicked Enable screen access

macOS’s message may have opened behind other windows. Look for it, or open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording and turn the app on there.

The app is missing from the Screen & System Audio Recording list

Click + below the list, choose the app from your Applications folder, turn it on, then quit and reopen the app.

A recording stopped by itself

This happens when the recorded window closes, a display is disconnected or changes resolution, the microphone is unplugged, or the Mac goes to sleep. The app saves what it recorded and tells you why it stopped.

A recording was not saved

The app never deletes recordings. Unfinished files are kept in a hidden folder named .MacScreenRecorderWork inside your recordings folder. Email us and we will help.

Privacy Policy

Screen Recorder – AI Ready for Mac · Last updated 23 September 2026

In short: Screen Recorder does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your recordings stay on your Mac. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything over the internet.

Who we are

Screen Recorder – AI Ready (“the app”) is made by NEX APP STUDIO LLC (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy, email us at screenrecorderappmac@gmail.com.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your recordings are saved as video files in the folder you choose (Movies › Recordings unless you pick another folder). We never receive them.
  • Small information files are kept next to each recording, for example its length, picture size and which screen, window or area was recorded. The app uses them to check and recover recordings. They stay in your recordings folder.
  • Your settings, such as the save folder, appearance, audio choices and whether the window stays on top, are kept in the app’s own storage on your Mac.
  • Screen previews shown inside the app are made on your Mac, kept only in memory and never saved or sent anywhere.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Screen & System Audio Recording lets the app record your screen, one window or an area you choose, and the Mac’s sound if you turn that on. The app records only when you press Start, or when an AI assistant you connected starts a recording (see below).
  • Microphone is used only if you turn the microphone on. “Test microphone” listens only while the test runs and saves nothing.
  • Camera is used only if you turn the camera on. The camera picture appears in a bubble on your screen and is recorded into your video on your Mac; it is never sent anywhere.
  • Folder access covers only the folder you choose for your recordings.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

AI assistants (optional)

AI assistants running on your Mac that support the Model Context Protocol (MCP) can control the app, but only after you turn on Allow local automation in the app’s Settings. Nothing listens while it is off. The connection stays on your Mac, and only programs running under your own user account can use it.

A connected assistant can see information such as the names and sizes of your displays, whether a recording is running, and where a finished recording was saved on your Mac. It can start recordings and stop the ones it started. It cannot stop, take over or get the file of a recording you started yourself.

The app itself never sends your recordings anywhere. An assistant, however, may be able to open a saved recording and may send information to its own online service. What an assistant does with information is covered by that assistant’s own privacy policy.

Keeping, deleting and changing your choices

Your recordings stay in the folder you chose until you delete them. Deleting a recording in Finder deletes it; we have no copy. The app’s settings stay on your Mac until you remove the app.

You can withdraw access at any time: turn off screen recording or microphone access for Screen Recorder in System Settings → Privacy & Security, and turn off Allow local automation in the app’s Settings to stop AI assistants from using it.

Problem reports (optional)

The app keeps a small activity log on your Mac to help fix problems. It contains no recordings, screen content, window titles or file names. Nothing is sent automatically: only if you choose Help › Report a Problem and then email the report to us yourself do we receive it, along with your Mac model, macOS version and any crash reports it includes. We use it only to fix the problem and delete it when we no longer need it.

Information Apple may share with us

If you choose to share analytics with app developers (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and basic usage statistics with us. We use them only to find and fix problems. Apple’s privacy policy explains how Apple collects this information.

Apple handles App Store downloads. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
